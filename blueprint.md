# nodd - Verified Young Creators Platform

## Overview
nodd is a platform designed to connect verified Gen-Z creators (high school and college students) with brands looking for fast, trendy, and AI-enhanced content (Shorts, Reels, TikTok).

## Features
- **Curated Creator Pool:** Only verified high school and university students.
- **Fast Delivery:** Average 48-hour delivery time.
- **AI-Enhanced Content:** Focus on modern AI editing tools and trends.
- **Fair Revenue Share:** 80% for creators, 20% platform fee.
- **Interactive UI:** Modern, responsive landing page with custom cursor, hover effects, and smooth animations.

## Design & Style
- **Typography:** Syne for headlines, DM Sans for body text.
- **Colors:** 
  - Accent: #b8e000 (Lime)
  - Dark Accent: #7aaa00
  - Background: #f7f5f0
  - Text: #111111
- **Visual Effects:** 
  - Background glow drifting animation.
  - Subtle noise texture.
  - Custom cursor and ring.
  - Reveal animations on scroll.
- **Components:** 
  - Floating hero card.
  - Marquee for features.
  - Grid-based steps and creator cards.
  - Comparison pricing table.
  - Modal-based Auth and Creator application.
